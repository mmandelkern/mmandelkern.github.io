Formal Approaches to Rationality and Meaning (FARM)
October 10-11, 2026
NYU Department of Philosophy, Room 202
5 Washington Place, New York, NY

Organized by Harvey Lederman and Matt Mandelkern (local), and Paolo Santorio, Julia Staffel, and Snow Zhang, with the generous support of NYU and the New York Institute of Philosophy

FARM is a conference that aims to bring together researchers studying meaning, reasoning and rational norms. This is the second iteration of the conference; information about last year's can be found here.
